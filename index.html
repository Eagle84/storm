Find your gaps before someone uninvited does

Defences are only proven the moment someone tries to get past them. Storm runs that test deliberately and hands the result to your defensive team.

Awareness testing

Simulated social-engineering campaigns that measure how your people respond, so training lands where it's needed.

Controlled exercises

Staged attack-path exercises run in an isolated lab, to validate whether a defence holds under a realistic sequence.

Campaign tracking

A console that records how an authorised engagement unfolds, so results are reviewable and reportable.

Feeds your defence

Findings hand straight off to Shield's workflow engine, turning an offensive result into a tracked defensive fix.

Authorised testing, always

Storm exists to make organisations harder to attack — never to attack anyone.

Scope and permission first. Storm is for engagements run with the explicit, written permission of the system owner, against systems and people agreed to be in-scope. Unauthorised use against anyone or anything you aren't permitted to test is illegal and is not a use this project supports. You are responsible for complying with all applicable laws.
